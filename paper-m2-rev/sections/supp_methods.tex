\paragraph{Data and inputs.} MIMIC-IV shifts each patient's dates by a random offset, so an approximate calendar year was recovered from the anchor-year group, with a resolution of about one year; the order of arrivals within a year is therefore not recoverable. Deaths after discharge are not captured by the outcome. Vital signs outside plausible ranges were set to missing, temperatures were converted to degrees Celsius, and each vital sign and static feature was standardized with the mean and standard deviation of the training subset. The comorbidity count uses a window of \RVElixWindowDays{} days before arrival (the window of the benchmark pipeline, about five years). Stays whose own linked admission appeared as a prior admission because of an inconsistent discharge time (\RVOwnAdmissionStays{} stays, \RVOwnAdmissionCohortChanged{} with a changed count in the analyzed cohort) were corrected in the landmark cohorts. The sequence tensors, outlier rules, and elapsed-time construction follow the repository of the benchmark.

\paragraph{Outcomes relative to the endpoint of the benchmark.} The primary outcome retains \RVRetainedPrimary{} and the secondary outcome \RVRetainedSecondary{} of the \RVOriginalPositivesTotal{} positives of the original endpoint, and the primary outcome also counts \RVOverlapPrimaryOnly{} ICU admissions later than 12 hours after ED departure but within 24 hours of the landmark.

\paragraph{Models and training.} The tree ensemble and logistic regression receive, for each vital sign, the mean, minimum, maximum, last value, and count, the four static features, and timing features: the number of ED rows, the time span and the intervals between measurements, the time since the last measurement overall and per vital sign, and counts of missing values; logistic regression uses median imputation and missing-value indicators. The neural models minimized binary cross-entropy without class weighting (Adam, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batches of 128 stays, early stopping on the validation loss with patience 10 and at most 100 epochs) on one NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU with PyTorch 2.13. The trees used XGBoost 3.4 with up to 1,000 trees and early stopping after 50 rounds on the validation AUROC; they use row and column subsampling, so their seeds also vary. Logistic regression, imputation, and standardization used scikit-learn 1.9, and logistic regression is deterministic.

\paragraph{Conformal methods.} The score of label $k$ is $1 - \hat{p}_k(x)$, which equals $|k - \hat{p}(x)|$ for a binary label. With score $s_t = |y_t - \hat{p}_t|$ and radius $q_t$, the radius of ECI is updated after each label:
\begin{align}
g_t &= \alpha\,\mathbf{1}\{q_t \ge s_t\} - (1-\alpha)\,\mathbf{1}\{q_t < s_t\}, \\
\tilde{q}_{t+1} &= \tilde{q}_t - \eta_t g_t, \quad q_{t+1} = \tilde{q}_{t+1} + \eta_t (s_t - q_t)\,\sigma'(s_t - q_t),
\end{align}
where $\sigma'$ is the derivative of the logistic function and $\eta_t$ is a base rate $\eta \in \{0.01, 0.05, 0.1\}$, chosen on the calibration-selection subset, times the range of the scores over the previous five steps. The reference implementation of ECI is the code of its authors, with one compatibility edit (the deprecated NumPy constant \texttt{np.infty} replaced by \texttt{np.inf}); our event-driven re-implementation agreed with it to numerical precision at zero delay. The scale of the logistic derivative is $c = 1$, as in that code. The burn-in window of five steps was fixed by our protocol; the reference experiments of the method use windows of \RVRefBurninLo{} to \RVRefBurninHi{} steps and base rates of \RVRefEtaLo{} to \RVRefEtaHi{}, so our settings differ from them. Because the order of arrivals within a year is not recoverable, the test stream reproduces the shift between the periods and the coarse year-to-year drift but not the true order of arrivals, which is why the drift test was imposed. We verified that the singleton $\{1\}$ of a static set is issued exactly for the stays whose score exceeds one threshold and compared its PPV and sensitivity with the precision of a plain threshold at the same sensitivity on the precision--recall curve of the primary model (seed 42). For the intercept recalibration, the logit of the score was shifted by an intercept estimated by maximum likelihood with the slope fixed at 1, on the most recent 10{,}000 labeled arrivals, and re-estimated every 2{,}000 arrivals; the quantile was then either recomputed for each block on the final-calibration scores shifted by the same intercept, or recomputed on the recalibrated scores every 2{,}000 arrivals from the most recent 10{,}000. NEWS2 used the last value of each parameter recorded by $L$.

\paragraph{Shortcut diagnostic.} The workflow set was fitted with ED duration (departure minus arrival) and its logarithm; further models used indicators of missing triage vital signs, both sets together, and ED duration alone. The regularization strength was selected on the validation subset by AUROC, and each model was evaluated once on the test period. The same protocol was applied to the triage acuity alone and to acuity with the workflow set, and each model, and Hybrid GRU-D for reference, was scored within acuity strata; a stratum with fewer than 20 events or 20 non-events was not evaluated. The end-of-stay models were also scored within quartiles of ED duration, with cut points from the training period, and refitted without the comorbidity count (the static branch and head of Hybrid GRU-D on its frozen encoder, and the tree ensemble).

\paragraph{Recording practice and the copied triage row.} The first ED vital-sign row counts as repeating the triage measurement when at least three of heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, and systolic and diastolic pressure are recorded in both and all of them are equal. In the de-duplicated refit this row was removed, the later rows were shifted up, and all features were rebuilt. When the stored recurrent models were scored on de-duplicated test stays, the elapsed-time input was rebuilt with the recursion of the data pipeline, which reproduced the stored elapsed-time input of the unmodified tensors, and the rule for a refit of Hybrid GRU-D, namely a change of the seed-mean AUROC of \RVGrudRule{} or more in either direction (about two thirds of the margin of the pre-specified contrast with the trees), was written down before the result was seen.

\paragraph{Analysis plan and literature search.} The analysis plan fixed, before any landmark model was trained, the cohort, outcomes, inputs, models, tuning budget, contrasts and significance rule, conformal methods, coverage levels, label delays, and drift test, together with the end-of-stay diagnostics (input audit, shortcut diagnostic, comorbidity ablation, and duration quartiles). The landmark-time comparison and its fixed set, the acuity-stratified shortcut diagnostic, calibration, reliability, the decision curve, discrimination by subgroup and calendar year, the cohort characteristics, the stream-order permutations, and the conformal suite on the two other models were added after the first results; the recording practice by year, the arrival information, the bootstrap intervals of the static coverage, the precision--recall comparison, and the intercept recalibration were added after an external review. For the literature search, one author screened the first 60 records of each query by title and, where available, abstract, and recorded which records addressed the same question and which reported the specific analysis. Every reported value is read from a stored result file through an accessor that records its source, and a separate step re-derives each value and compares it with the manuscript assets.
