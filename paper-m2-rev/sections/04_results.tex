\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Study Cohort}
\label{sec:results-cohort}

At the two-hour landmark, \RVHtwoTestEligible{} of the \RVHtwoTestStays{} test-period stays were eligible and \RVHtwoTestPositives{} had the primary outcome (Table~\ref{tab:cohort}).
The primary-outcome rate rose from \RVCharTrainPrimary{} in the training period to \RVCharTestPrimary{} in the test period, and the share of stays with at least one comorbidity fell from \RVCharTrainElixPct{} to \RVCharTestElixPct{}.

The recording of vital signs changed between the periods (Table~\ref{tab:cohort}). In an added analysis (Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:recording}), the change was a transition between 2013 and 2016 that ended before the test period. The share of eligible stays with no ED vital sign by two hours fell from \RVRecZeroC{} in 2011 to \RVRecZeroF{} in 2014 and \RVRecZeroH{} in 2016, and over the same years the share of stays whose first ED vital-sign row repeats the triage measurement rose from \RVRecEqTriC{} to \RVRecEqTriF{} and \RVRecEqTriH{}, and was \RVRecEqTriTest{} in the test period.

Removing the copied row left the discrimination of the classical models unchanged (added analysis; Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:dedup}). It was removed for \RVDdFlaggedFit{} of the stays of the fitting subset and \RVDdFlaggedTest{} of the test period, and the AUROC of the trees and of logistic regression changed by \RVDdTreesChange{} (\RVDdTreesChangeLo{} to \RVDdTreesChangeHi{}) and \RVDdLrChange{} (\RVDdLrChangeLo{} to \RVDdLrChangeHi{}). Removing the timing features changed them by \RVNtTreesChange{} and \RVNtLrChange{}, and the advantage of the trees over logistic regression was \RVOrigTreesOverLr{} (\RVOrigTreesOverLrLo{} to \RVOrigTreesOverLrHi{}) in the primary analysis and between \RVNtTreesOverLr{} and \RVVoTreesOverLr{} in the three variants. Scoring the stored Hybrid GRU-D models on test stays without the copied row, with no refit, changed the seed-mean AUROC from \RVGrudHybridStored{} to \RVGrudHybridDd{} (\RVGrudHybridChange{}; \RVGrudHybridChangeLo{} to \RVGrudHybridChangeHi{}), below the threshold of \RVGrudRule{} set beforehand, so the refit was not run; the Temporal-Only models changed by \RVGrudTempChange{}.

\subsection{Timing of the Inputs and the Shortcut Diagnostic}
\label{sec:results-audit}

The end-of-stay models read the last ten vital-sign rows of the stay, so the rows extend past any early prediction time: in the test period,
\RVAfterTwoHTest{} of stays kept a row charted more than two hours after arrival, and \RVAfterDepRowsTest{} retained rows in \RVAfterDepStaysTest{} test stays were charted after ED departure (Table~\ref{tab:timing}). The comorbidity count passed the audit; removing it changed the AUROC of Hybrid GRU-D by \RVElixDeltaHybrid{} and of the tree ensemble by \RVElixDeltaXgb{}
(Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:elixhauser}).

The workflow model, a logistic regression on ED duration, the number of measurements, and the per-vital measurement counts, reached an AUROC of \RVShortAAuroc{} (95\% CI
\RVShortALo{}--\RVShortAHi{}) on the end-of-stay task, against
\RVEosHybridAuroc{} for Hybrid GRU-D and \RVEosXgbAuroc{} for the tree ensemble on the whole-stay vital signs (Table~\ref{tab:shortcuts}). Arrival information alone, which is known before any monitoring, reached \RVIncArr{} (\RVIncArrLo{}--\RVIncArrHi{}) in an added analysis, and the workflow quantities added to it increased the AUROC to \RVIncArrWork{} (\RVIncArrWorkLo{}--\RVIncArrWorkHi{}), an increase of \RVIncWorkOverArr{} (\RVIncWorkOverArrLo{} to \RVIncWorkOverArrHi{}); ED duration added \RVIncDurOverArr{}.

\subsection{Discrimination and Calibration at the Landmark}
\label{sec:results-discrimination}

Landmark values are not comparable with end-of-stay values, which come from other inputs, another outcome, and a larger population. The timing-aware tree ensemble
reached an AUROC of \RVAurocXgbT{} (95\% CI \RVLoXgbT{}--\RVHiXgbT{}), the end-to-end Hybrid GRU-D \RVAurocEndToEnd{} (\RVLoEndToEnd{}--\RVHiEndToEnd{}), and logistic
regression \RVAurocLr{} (\RVLoLr{}--\RVHiLr{}) (Table~\ref{tab:discrimination}); the ranking was the same for the secondary outcome. The Temporal-Only GRU-D reached
\RVAurocTmp{} and NEWS2 \RVNewsHtwo{}.
All models predicted a lower mean risk than the observed rate (\RVCalMeanPredEndToEnd{} against \RVCalObserved{} for the primary model), as the positive intercepts show; the calibration slopes were close to 1.

Among the pre-specified contrasts (adjusted values in Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:contrasts}), the primary model scored \RVCOneDelta{} (\RVCOneLo{} to \RVCOneHi{}) relative to
the tree ensemble and \RVCTwoDelta{} (\RVCTwoLo{} to \RVCTwoHi{}) relative to logistic regression; both met the significance rule. The contrast with the Temporal-Only
model, which lacks the static features, was \RVCThreeDelta{}, and that with the frozen-encoder variant was \RVCFourDelta{}, which did not meet the all-seeds rule. At the arrival landmark the tree ensemble exceeded logistic regression by \RVCFiveDelta{}
(\RVCFiveLo{} to \RVCFiveHi{}). By approximate calendar year of the test period, the tree ensemble was at or above the
primary model in every year (extended version). The arrival-landmark models scored on the \RVCommonN{} test stays eligible at the two-hour landmark, against the two-hour outcome, reached \RVCommonArrTrees{} (trees) against \RVCommonTwoTrees{} for the two-hour trees, a gain of \RVCommonTreesGain{} (\RVCommonTreesGainLo{} to \RVCommonTreesGainHi{}); for logistic regression the gain was \RVCommonLrGain{}. AUROC was lower at 65 years or older (\RVSubAgeOverAuroc{}) and for triage acuity 3 (\RVSubAcuityMidAuroc{}) than below 65 years (\RVSubAgeUnderAuroc{}) and for acuity 1 or 2 (\RVSubAcuityHighAuroc{}) (extended version).

\subsection{Conformal Prediction on the Later Period}
\label{sec:results-coverage}

\subsubsection{Marginal Coverage}
Static split-conformal score intervals, calibrated on \RVNFc{} final-calibration stays (\RVPosFc{} with the outcome), covered \RVIntCovStatic{} (SD \RVIntCovSdStatic{}
over seeds) of the \RVNTest{} test stays at a target of 0.90, above the validity threshold of 0.89, with a mean width of \RVIntWidStatic{}.
ECI covered \RVIntCovEci{} at a width of \RVIntWidEci{}, and recalibration with a 10{,}000-arrival window \RVIntCovRecalTen{} at \RVIntWidRecalTen{}. In an added analysis, the patient-cluster bootstrap 95\% interval of the static coverage of the primary model was \RVStaticBootHybLo{} to \RVStaticBootHybHi{}, which includes the threshold of 0.89 (\RVStaticBootHybShare{} of resamples reached it). With the tree ensemble static coverage was \RVOtherXgbStaticCov{} (\RVStaticBootXgbLo{} to \RVStaticBootXgbHi{}; \RVStaticBootXgbShare{} of resamples reached the threshold), which does not meet the validity threshold, and with logistic regression \RVOtherLrStaticCov{}; ECI coverage was \RVOtherXgbEciCov{} and \RVOtherLrEciCov{}.

\subsubsection{Coverage of the Patients Who Deteriorated}
Marginal coverage did not extend to the patients who deteriorated (Table~\ref{tab:conformal}). Class-1 coverage was \RVSetCovOneStaticLac{} for static LAC sets and
\RVSetCovOneEciSets{} for ECI sets. A stay enters the marginal set with label 1 only if its predicted risk is at least \RVScoreNeeded{}, whereas the
median predicted risk among stays with the outcome was \RVScoreMedEvents{}. The singleton $\{1\}$ of a static set is issued exactly for the stays whose score exceeds one threshold, so its PPV and sensitivity are a point on the precision--recall curve of the score.

Mondrian calibration raised class-1 coverage to \RVSetCovOneStaticMond{} (static), \RVSetCovOneMondEci{} (Mondrian-ECI), and \RVSetCovOneMondRecal{} (recalibrated),
at mean set sizes of \RVSetSizeStaticMond{}, \RVSetSizeMondEci{}, and \RVSetSizeMondRecal{}. In an added analysis, for static Mondrian-LAC the PPV was \RVPrMondPpv{} at a sensitivity of \RVPrMondSens{}, against \RVPrMondSame{} for a plain threshold at that sensitivity, and for Mondrian-ECI \RVPrEciPpv{} at \RVPrEciSens{}, against \RVPrEciSame{}.

\subsubsection{Coverage Levels, Label Delay, and Drift}
\label{sec:results-alpha}
Across nominal coverage levels (Fig.~\ref{fig:alpha}, Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:alpha}), static LAC covered \RVATwentyCovOneStaticLac{} of the patients with the outcome at 0.80
and \RVAOneCovOneStaticLac{} at 0.99. Mondrian-ECI covered \RVATwentyCovOneMondEci{} of them at 0.80, with a PPV of \RVATwentyPpvMondEci{} and \RVATwentyFaMondEci{}
false alarms per 100 patients; at 0.99
its PPV was \RVAOnePpvMondEci{} with \RVAOneFaMondEci{} false alarm per 100 patients and a mean set size of \RVAOneSizeMondEci{}. The class-1 quantile was feasible at
every level.

\label{sec:results-robustness}
Delaying labels by 60 to 1{,}000 arrivals left ECI coverage between \RVLagSixtyEciCov{} and \RVLagThousandEciCov{}, recalibration at \RVLagSixtyRecalCov{}, and the
class-1 coverage of Mondrian-ECI at \RVLagSixtyMondEciCovOne{} and \RVLagThousandMondEciCovOne{} for the shortest and longest delay (Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:lag-drift}). Under imposed drift, static coverage fell to \RVDriftShiftOneStaticPost{} after a logit
shift of $+1.0$ and from \RVDriftCasemixStaticPre{} to \RVDriftCasemixStaticPost{} after the case-mix change. ECI held \RVDriftShiftOneEciPost{} and
\RVDriftCasemixEciPost{}, whereas recalibration held the logit shifts but reached \RVDriftCasemixRecalPost{} under the case-mix change.

In an added analysis, shifting the intercept of the score of the primary model on the most recent labelled arrivals raised its mean predicted risk from \RVIrMeanPredOrig{} to \RVIrMeanPredRecal{} (observed \RVIrObserved{}). When the same shift was applied to the final-calibration scores and the static quantile was recomputed, marginal coverage was \RVIrCovOne{} against \RVIrCovOrig{} without recalibration, and the class-1 coverage of marginal sets \RVIrOneOne{} against \RVIrOneOrig{}; with a quantile recomputed every 2{,}000 arrivals the values were \RVIrCovTwo{} and \RVIrOneTwo{}.
